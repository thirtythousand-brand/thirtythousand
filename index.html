Principles

We know you don't have time.

01

Fabrics that you can feel

Our fabric choices are all-natural, chosen for their task. Our designs are as much engineering as they are creative flair.

We know the sourcing of all our fabrics, we check the factories and mills for attention to detail and labour conditions — it all reflects in the garments you wear.

02

Design that works with you

When you're taking that call on the move, getting in and out of a car, when you're heading into the board room, you don't have time to think about the cut of your pants or whether your collar is folded properly. We get that, because we've done what you do.

Ruthless attention to detail rooted in experiencing the life you lead.

03

Anti-obnoxious

You're past gaudy LVMH trinkets; you don't need that validation. You validate yourself on your experience and your success.

These clothes are exclusive, constrained by limited drops responding to your needs.
