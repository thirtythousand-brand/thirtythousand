The Hero Tee

主打 T 恤

The Hero Tee is cut from 220gsm organic cotton with a longer back hem and side vents, so it keeps its shape through heat, humidity and long flights. Each colour is capped at 300 units per run.

主打 T 恤采用 220gsm 有机棉，后摆加长并设侧开衩，在炎热、潮湿和长途飞行中依然保持版型。每批单色限量不超过 300 件。

Principles

品牌信念

01

Natural fabrics 天然面料

Every fabric is natural and chosen for the job the garment has to do. We know where each one comes from and we check the mills and factories for workmanship and labour conditions.

每种面料都是天然材质，并根据服装的用途挑选。我们清楚每种面料的来源，并实地检查纱厂和工厂的工艺与用工状况。

02

Design for your day 为你的一天而设计

Our designs allow for taking calls on the move, getting in and out of cars and walking into a boardroom without adjusting a collar. We have lived that routine ourselves, so we check every detail against it.

我们的设计考虑到边走边接电话、频繁上下车，以及走进董事会议室时无需整理领口。我们自己也过着这样的生活，所以每个细节都会对照这种日常来检查。

03

Limited drops 限量发售

Each drop is limited and made in response to what our customers ask for. The clothes carry no logos, which leaves the wearer's own record to speak for them.

每一批都限量生产，并根据客户的需求来制作。衣服上没有任何 Logo，让穿着者自身的成就来说明一切。
