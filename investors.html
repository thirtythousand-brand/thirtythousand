Snapshot · 项目一览

The business at a glance

业务概览

Customers 客户

Our customers are professionals aged 28 to 45 who divide their time between airports, boardrooms and cities, and who value clothes that save them time.

我们的客户是 28 至 45 岁的专业人士，时间分配在机场、会议室和各个城市之间，看重能为自己节省时间的衣服。

Launch line 首发系列

The first line is the Hero Tee in 220gsm organic cotton, with a longer back hem and side vent, alongside an indigo-dyed tote that carries the brand story.

首发系列包括采用 220gsm 有机棉、后摆加长并设侧开衩的主打 T 恤，以及一款承载品牌故事的靛蓝染托特包。

Go-to-market 市场路径

We start on Instagram and Shopify with waitlist-led drops and focused pop-ups in Hong Kong, Brisbane and Singapore. For silhouette we benchmark against COS and Uniqlo U, and for transparency against Everlane.

我们先从 Instagram 和 Shopify 起步，通过候补名单限量发售，并在香港、布里斯班和新加坡举办定向快闪。在版型上我们对标 COS 和 Uniqlo U，在透明度上对标 Everlane。

Why now

为何是现在

Demand 需求

Customers are moving toward fewer, better pieces in natural fibres, with consistent fits that cut the time they spend deciding what to wear.

消费者正转向数量更少、品质更好的服装，偏好天然纤维和稳定的版型，以减少挑选衣服所花的时间。

Supply 供应

We work with audited partners in South China, cap each run at 300 units per colour and source yarn from South China and Turkey.

我们与华南经审核的工厂合作，每批单色限量不超过 300 件，纱线来自华南和土耳其。

Pricing 价格与价值

Small batches support our margins, and transparent sourcing builds the trust that leads to repeat purchases.

小批量生产支撑我们的利润空间，透明的溯源则建立起促成复购的信任。

We sell fewer and better pieces, and we show customers where each one comes from.

我们销售数量更少、品质更好的服装，并让客户看到每一件的来源。

Investment case

投资逻辑

The business 现状

Thirty Thousand is a lean, founder-led business with a working supply chain, tested prototypes and early community traction, and it is set up to grow profitably without outside capital.

Thirty Thousand 是一家精简的创始人主导企业，拥有运转中的供应链、经过测试的样品和早期的社群支持，并具备在没有外部资金的情况下实现盈利增长的结构。

The raise 融资

A single investment of around USD 0.5m would extend our reach, product range and marketing efficiency. Our prospectus projects an eight-fold annual return by years 3 to 4, assuming disciplined execution and realistic sell-through.

约 50 万美元的一次性投资将扩大我们的触达范围、产品深度和营销效率。根据说明书中的测算，在稳健执行和合理售罄率的前提下，该投入有望在第 3 至 4 年带来约 8 倍的年回报。

Structure 架构

Our design IP and administration sit in Hong Kong, while early production runs through trusted partners in South China. We plan to add premium sourcing in Australia, which gives investors exposure to a diversified, international operation.

我们的设计知识产权和行政管理位于香港，早期生产通过华南值得信赖的合作伙伴完成。我们计划在澳大利亚增加高端面料采购，为投资者带来多元化的国际业务布局。
